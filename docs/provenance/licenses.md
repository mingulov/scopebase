## Schema 2.0.0 declarations and copy gate

Each `adoption` record states disposition, copy/decision status, selected source scope, ownership basis, actual artifact role, destination license, authorization reference, third-party fragments and a reason when unresolved/reference-only. A selection is `FILE` or sorted, nonoverlapping inclusive positive line ranges. Explicit nulls mean unselected or unknown; `third_party_terms: null` differs from the explicit declaration `[]` that the selected scope contains no upstream portion. Unknown fields and malformed records fail validation.

`READY_FOR_SELECTED_SCOPE` requires a selected scope/role/destination, resolved ownership and every upstream option/evidence record. User-owned and mixed selections require the owner authorization and its role grant. Pure upstream selections retain the selected original option across their full scope; the owner's grant cannot replace upstream terms. A structurally ready declaration is not evidence that its claimed classification is complete or correct. The controller/expert must inspect the actual selected slice before dispatch or copying.

`NEEDS_DECISION` preserves unselected or unclassified future inputs with `NOT_COPIED`, explicit nulls and a concrete missing-decision reason. `REFERENCE_ONLY` requires `BLOCKED`, a reference/product-local disposition, a null destination and a technical/reference reason. Both states preserve knowledge without admitting a source copy. Neither blocks unrelated host work or foundation acceptance.

Of 128 observed inputs, sixteen narrow scopes are `READY_FOR_SELECTED_SCOPE` for LX02/CP02/LX04, 109 remain `NEEDS_DECISION`, and three MojoBPF compiler implementation files stay product-local/reference-only because the plan reuses their emitted objects and regressions rather than importing a compiler. The ready scopes retain `NOT_COPIED` as pre-copy admission state at their bound snapshot; readiness does not assert an actual port. The earlier automatic block on 14 `NOASSERTION` inputs is superseded by this model; their observed SPDX values and evidence are unchanged, and their unselected scopes now require a decision rather than a copying ban inferred from a label.

A separate version 1 [selected-port receipt](adoption-decisions.md#selected-port-receipts) records actual selected destination bytes against a retained ready admission snapshot. It preserves schema 2.0.0 source observations, original authorizations and pre-copy flags. Destination/preservation hashes bind observations, not authenticity, ownership or license correctness; independent review checks scope correspondence, regression preservation and applicable notices. Replacement receipts append a bound supersedes reference rather than rewriting earlier evidence. An intake-only check cannot infer no copying occurred or accept a port.

## Retained upstream options

The frozen [SB01 record contract](../superpowers/plans/2026-10-18-scopebase-plan-repair.md#sb01-adoption-record-contract) supports a deliberately small set. Single observed Apache-2.0, GPL-2.0-only, GPL-3.0-or-later and Apache-2.0 WITH LLVM-exception expressions retain that exact option. GPL-2.0-or-later also permits GPL-2.0-only; MIT OR Apache-2.0 permits either offered branch. `NOASSERTION` supplies no third-party grant; unfamiliar expressions remain observed and require a decision.

Ready host/build-tool/data-document upstream portions support Apache-2.0 and Apache-2.0 WITH LLVM-exception. Ready GPLv2 BPF portions support GPL-2.0-only and GPL-2.0-or-later. Shared host/BPF source can be ready with only the owner's dual grant; any shared upstream portion requires a decision in this version. An upstream MIT branch remains subject to the unresolved dependency-policy choice. Aya target intake must not silently elect MIT or bare Apache for GPLv2 output. The supplied LLVM exception must be retained; BPF/shared use requires separate review of the actual fragments and exception. These conservative admission checks are not a general SPDX compatibility engine or a prohibition on all other upstream use.

Each upstream fragment has a contained, nonoverlapping scope, original offered expression, selected option or null, and license/notice evidence paths and SHA-256 values. It inherits the parent's source pin/hash and cannot substitute a foreign binding. Selected options require license evidence. Empty notice evidence declares no separate notice requirement; expert review checks that declaration and later packaging checks delivery. Structural range/evidence validation does not inspect the source text or prove every upstream portion was declared.
