# TT07 delivery fixture specification (frozen v1)

Registered fixture source for `TT07.closure` and `TT07.binding` under the
`operator-delivery-v1` profile.

Fixtures are authored vectors served from the verified closure: frozen
input/expected files plus per-run ledger records regenerated by the
operator with the fresh run id. The closure manifest pins every byte the
route may read; the fixture root is never written. Truth kind is
`fixed_before_execute` at tier T0.
